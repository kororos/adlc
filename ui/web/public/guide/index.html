Agentic Delivery Life Cycle

From an idea to merged pull requests, with you deciding what matters.

ADLC runs Claude agents on your dev server. You plan the product with Claude in four conversations; then a poller delivers the GitHub issues one by one, in dependency order, and only comes back to you for decisions, high-risk reviews and your own tasks. Pick a step to see who does what.
